Backup & DR readiness

A starter structure for preparing a recovery-readiness discussion.

Outline

  1. Identify systems and recovery priorities.
  2. Review backup scope, storage, and retention requirements.
  3. Document recovery roles and communication paths.
  4. Plan a controlled recovery test.
  5. Record findings and update the recovery documentation.
